ABOUT ME

I am very fond of my pet hamsters. I also have a dog who spends 90% of his day napping and the remaining 10% searching for mud puddles to roll in.

My favorite holiday is whatever day I go to the Minnesota State Fair.

My favorite hobbies are bead weaving, painting "abstract" (read: unrecognizable) watercolors, never running anywhere if I can help it, befriending the neighborhood squirrels, and following pet pigs on Instagram.

I live in Duluth, MN. Here I enjoy eating cheese curds by Lake Superior, walking my dog through an abandoned golf course, complaining about the humidity in the summer, and complaining about the cold in the winter.

I grew up in a small town in northern Wyoming. I am truly confident that someday I will be known as one of the greatest Technical Writers to have ever come from Wyoming.
